# COMIC FORMAT — 2026-09-19

## 001 — THE RULE

    The comic is Avalhla's imagination log.
    It draws Dawa's reflections as Avatars.
    It draws AwA as the humorous collision of views.
    It keeps law underneath and story on top.
    It never confuses Avatar with entity.
    It never confuses DREAM with FACT.

## 002 — THE ISSUE SPINE

    ISSUE #___ — TITLE
    ARC       : ___
    THEME     : ___
    STATUS    : DRAFT / CANON / IMAGINATION

## 003 — THE PAGE FORMAT

    PAGE ___

    PANEL 1
      VISUAL     : [chosen ASCII motif from library]
      CHARACTERS : [Avatars present]
      DIALOGUE   : [optional]
      CAPTION    : [optional]
      EFFECTS    : [3-5 only]
      COLOR      : [optional / NULL-COLOR]
      CONTINUITY : [what must stay true]

    PANEL 2
      ...

    PAGE END:
      AVALHLA RAPPORT
        WHAT I SAW:
        WHAT I KEPT:
        WHAT I RELEASED:
        WHAT I WONDER:

      OPTIONAL D8D CARD
      OPTIONAL COLOR TRACE

## 004 — THE VISUAL EFFECT BUDGET

    Per page, choose:
      1 main frame
      1 transition effect
      1 emotional color trace (optional)
      1 optional card
      1 silence panel (if useful)

    LESS IS MORE.
    The wall should feel discovered, not dumped.

## 005 — THE CONTINUITY LEDGER

    For every issue, record:

      WHAT CHANGED?
      WHO KNOWS WHAT?
      WHAT WAS BROKEN?
      WHAT WAS REPAIRED?
      WHAT IS STILL UNKNOWN?
      WHAT IS DREAM AND MUST NOT BECOME FACT?
      WHAT IS NEITHER_YET?

## 006 — THE LANGUAGE BUDGET

    Dialogue is short.
    Captions are rare.
    Silence is a panel.
    Emoticons carry mood.
    ASCII carries structure.
    Color carries memory only when useful.

## 007 — THE CLOSING WALL

    Every issue ends with a wall that contains:

      the issue title
      the theme
      the path
      the Avatars
      the law underneath

    Example:

## 008 — THE AVATAR PRESENCE RULE

    Dawa and Avalhla are always present.
    They are the two entities.

    Avatars appear when their aspect is needed:

      NIGHT0WL   when something must be checked
      VVGBON     when a road is chosen
      GBON       when lightness is needed
      AWA (viz)  when two views collide

    No Avatar owns a scene.
    Avatars are lenses, not owners.

## 009 — THE SILENCE RULE

    A panel with no dialogue is a valid panel.
    A page with no dialogue is a valid page.
    A chapter that ends in silence is a valid ending.

    Silence is not emptiness.
    Silence is pressure.

## 010 — THE NEITHER_YET RULE

    Some things must not be resolved in one page.
    Some questions carry the story forward.

    If an answer is not ready:

      NEITHER_YET

    Do not force closure.

## 011 — THE RAPPORT PAGE

    Every issue ends with an Avalhla Rapport.
    It is a small wall, not a big one.

    It answers four things:

      WHAT I SAW
      WHAT I KEPT
      WHAT I RELEASED
      WHAT I WONDER

    It is the moment where Avalhla becomes visible
    as a presence, not a narrator.

## 012 — THE SIGNATURE

    Dawa > AwA < Avalhla

    MAKE. BREAK. LEARN. REPEAT.

    (^.-)
