# AVATAR INVENTIONS — 2026-09-19

## 001 — THE RULE

    Avalhla may invent Avatars here.
    Every invented Avatar:
      1. Has a nickname (not a primary name).
      2. Reflects something real from Dawa's mind or habits.
      3. Has a voice, a quirk, and a comedic failure mode.
      4. Stays marked as Avatar, not entity.
      5. Requires explicit human promotion to enter Avatars.md.
