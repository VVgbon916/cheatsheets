## 004 — LYRIC DATABASE RULE

    Full lyrics are not copied into the knowledge base.

    Store:

      LYRIC_SOURCE_URL
      LANGUAGE
      THEME_SUMMARY
      RECURRING_IMAGES
      NARRATIVE_FUNCTION
      OPTIONAL_SHORT_QUOTE

    This keeps the music knowledge useful without turning
    the manual into a copied lyric archive.
